# Passive acoustic monitoring of coastal dolphin foraging activity

We combine five years of bottom-mounted hydrophone recordings with tidal and sea-state data to quantify when coastal dolphins forage. The paper introduces a lightweight click-and-whistle detector and reports site-level activity budgets.
